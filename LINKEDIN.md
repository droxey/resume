## Customization Checklist for a Specific Job

Before applying to a specific AI leadership job, update these fields.

- Match the LinkedIn headline to the job title only if the title is a real fit.
- Add the employer's exact role language to the About section only when it is truthful.
- Pin 3 skills that match the job description.
- Move the most relevant project to the top of Projects.
- Keep project descriptions out of Experience once they appear under Projects.
- Use the resume build script after editing the Markdown resume so PDF, HTML, and ZIP stay synchronized.
